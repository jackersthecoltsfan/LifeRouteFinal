LifeRoute UI/AI reliability TestFlight release requested after successful iOS build, full regression audit, and web-preview validation.
Release source: bd164606976d695fb15f130c195e6c222fe3abd2

Included release-hardening changes:
- Generate Day uses only localized verified schedule facts; no invented meals, cafes, stops, times, or route suggestions.
- Route insight remains deterministic from LifeRoute/MapKit data, with recommendations kept out of factual Generate Day prose.
- Metallic Wave uses the same preview-card visual grammar as Dynamic / Fluid themes.
- iPhone WKWebView zoom is locked to the app shell and form fields avoid focus zoom.
- ABA AI note quality/audits are aligned with the grounded narrative-first v2 prompt.

This test build intentionally uses Apple-native intelligence only: Apple Foundation Models for on-device language/planning tasks, Apple Vision for screenshot OCR/visual analysis, and Apple Image Playground where supported. Deterministic LifeRoute systems remain authoritative for calendar events, route/travel calculations, stop duration, departure timing, reminders, and other exact app data.
